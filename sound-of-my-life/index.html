Sound of My Life

A melodic tune to sing and dance along to. A song for young spirits on the path to self-discovery.

"Sound of My Life" has been an important part of Ceramic Doves' lead singer Dana. She wrote the piano melody as a child and is sharing it now with the world, together with the life lessons she has learned since childhood. These lessons have accompanied her ever since — much like this lovely piano melody.

Bio

Bio

The young Swiss rock band Ceramic Doves is making waves. Guitar-driven rock'n'roll with depth, glamour, and a show that hits straight to the heart and the legs. «Young hearts rock hard» – and that's exactly how they sound: loud, direct, and on a straight path to the world tour.

Artist Bio

Ceramic Doves is a rock band from the Zürcher Oberland. Founded in 2023, the band consists of Dana (vocals), Ian Carlson (guitar, vocals), Lisa Gehrig (keyboard, vocals), Gian Curiger (bass) and Ian Margelisch (drums). With their guitar-driven sound the band quickly built a growing live presence and released their first EP in 2024. In their current lineup, Ceramic Doves took third place at the Zurich emerging-talent competition Band-it in 2025.

That same year, the singles "Painting of My Love" and "Diane" were released, the latter recorded at Powerplay Studios in Maur. Alongside this, the band played further concerts in regional clubs and music bars. Behind the energetic stage show are songs that grapple with self-discovery, relationships, and coming of age. Besides appearances at various Swiss festivals, 2026 brings the band's first shows abroad, in Germany and Austria.

Ceramic Doves is a co-founder of the musicians' collective soundinme.ch from Uster, which supports the local music scene through joint projects and events.
