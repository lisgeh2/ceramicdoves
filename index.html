About

Five people, one long night.

Ceramic Doves began in a rented basement on the edge of the city, where four friends spent winter nights turning insomnia into songs. What started as an experiment in reverb and patience became a sound of its own — spacious, unhurried, and quietly restless.

Since our first EP Love Rehab in 2021, we've toured smoky rooms and open fields alike, always chasing that thin, grey light that arrives just before the sun. This is music for the drive home, the empty platform, the last hour awake.
