About

underground to central stage.

The young Swiss rock band Ceramic Doves is making waves. Guitar-driven rock’n’roll with depth, glamour, and a show that hits straight to the heart and the legs. «Young hearts rock hard» – and that’s exactly how they sound: loud, direct, and on a straight path to the world tour.

The band was founded in 2023 and consists of Dana (vocals), Ian Carlson (guitar, vocals), Lisa Gehrig (keyboard, vocals), Gian Curiger (bass), and Ian Margelisch (drums). Soon they released their first EP in 2024. In their current lineup, Ceramic Doves took third place at the Zurich emerging-talent competition Band-it in 2025. That same year, the singles "Painting of My Love" and "Diane" were released, the latter recorded at Powerplay Studios in Maur. Alongside this, the band played further concerts in regional clubs and music bars. Behind the energetic stage show are songs that grapple with themes such as self-discovery, relationships, and coming of age.
